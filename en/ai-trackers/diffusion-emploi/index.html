About this tracker

This dashboard tracks the diffusion of artificial intelligence in the French economy in real time through the analysis of job postings.

Indicators tracked

  • Total AI job postings : cumulative number of job postings mentioning AI skills
  • Generative vs. traditional AI : distinction between job postings targeting generative AI (GPT, LLMs, etc.) and traditional AI (ML, vision, NLP)
  • Sector distribution : breakdown between industry and services
  • Occupations (ROME) : classification of job postings by occupational family (ROME, the French repertoire of occupations)
  • Technical skills : trend in the technologies in demand (TensorFlow, PyTorch, etc.)
  • Geography : breakdown by employment zone

Methodology

The analysis relies on:

  1. Collection : continuous extraction of job postings from the JOCAS database (DARES)
  2. ML classification : machine learning models trained to detect mentions of AI
  3. Categorization : automatic distinction between generative AI, computer vision, NLP, etc.
  4. Aggregation : computation of monthly and quarterly indicators
