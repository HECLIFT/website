Antonin Bergeaud

Antonin Bergeaud

Co-director

Antonin Bergeaud is an associate professor in the Economics Department of HEC Paris and a research affiliate of the LSE’s Centre for Economic Performance, the CEPR and the Collège de France’s Innovation Lab. His research focuses on long-run growth, productivity dynamics and the role of innovation in the diffusion of knowledge. He received the 2025 Best Young Economist Prize (Le Monde / Cercle des économistes).
