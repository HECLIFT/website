About this tracker

This dashboard tracks inventive activity in high-technology sectors through international patent filings (PCT). It measures the contribution of each country and region to key technologies (AI, biotechnology, information technology, green technologies and semiconductors) since the 1980s.

Author

This tracker is developed by Antonin Bergeaud — HEC Paris, LSE, CEPR.

Methodology

Source data

The patents analyzed are international applications filed under the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT) of the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO). The data are extracted from Google Patents Public Data via BigQuery. The PCT covers filings in more than 150 member countries and is a reliable indicator of international inventive activity.

Fractional counting

Each patent is attributed to its inventors’ countries fractionally using the OECD RegPat database. If a patent has 3 inventors (2 in France, 1 in Germany), France receives 2/3 of the patent and Germany 1/3. This method avoids double counting and reflects each country’s actual contribution.

Geographic entities

  • EU-27 : The 27 current member states of the European Union (post-Brexit)
  • Europe : EU-27 + Norway, Switzerland, United Kingdom
  • United States, China, Japan : individual countries
  • Rest of the world : All other countries (the composition varies with the EU/Europe mode selected)

Shares are computed on the world total (including all entities). The 5 entities sum to 100% each year.

Data sources

  • Google Patents Public Data : PCT publications (WO) with IPC and CPC classifications
  • OECD RegPat (August 2026 edition): Inventor register with fractional geographic attribution
  • IPC classification : International Patent Classification (WIPO)
  • CPC classification : Cooperative Patent Classification (EPO/USPTO)
